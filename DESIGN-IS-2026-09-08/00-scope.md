# Design audit scope

- **Live URL:** https://asifuddin.com/
- **Repository:** `/Users/mdasifuddin/Assignment_pdf/portfolio`
- **Audited surface:** Public portfolio homepage and its primary navigation/destination pages, checked at representative desktop and mobile viewport sizes; source components and styles are included where they explain shipped behavior.
- **Primary user:** A potential collaborator, employer, client, or academically inclined visitor evaluating Asifuddin's work.
- **Primary task:** Quickly understand who Asifuddin is, inspect credible examples of his work and thinking, and find a clear way to continue or make contact.
- **Constraints:** Preserve the site's scholarly/personal character and existing content; Astro 7 and Tailwind 4 stack; responsive behavior; WCAG 2.2 AA as the accessibility floor; no stated deadline.
- **References/competitors:** None supplied. The audit evaluates the shipped design on its own terms rather than imposing a different visual genre.
- **Excluded:** Admin/CMS editing flows, exhaustive proofreading of every long-form article, and implementation of the broader redesign recommendations. The separately requested Frontispiece/Elementa mobile-motion repair is included and verified.
