the trade, stated twice
  quadrupling B halves the noise and costs 4x the arithmetic
  B=1 -> B=4     noise 1.0000 -> 0.5000  (2.0x better, 4x cost)
  B=4 -> B=16    noise 0.5000 -> 0.2500  (2.0x better, 4x cost)

critical batch: gradient noise against the gradient itself
       B   noise/signal                             regime
       1       1.000000 noise-dominated: more B helps a lot
      16       0.250000 mixed: linear scaling roughly holds
     256       0.062500 mixed: linear scaling roughly holds
    4096       0.015625 signal-dominated: more B buys little
   65536       0.003906 signal-dominated: more B buys little

what that means for a fixed compute budget
  budget 1,000,000 example-visits
  B=   32     31,250 steps   noise/step 0.17678
  B=  256      3,906 steps   noise/step 0.06250
  B= 2048        488 steps   noise/step 0.02210
