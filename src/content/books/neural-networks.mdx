Book I ends with a gated recurrence still failing on a long dependency. That
failure is not a training problem and no initialisation repairs it. Book II
opens by asking what would have to be true to fix it.

Everything here is deliberately domain-neutral. If a derivation would read
identically whether the data were images, text or gene expression, it belongs
in this book. Attention, position and the Transformer block do not: they are
the foundation of everything from Book III onwards, and they are reached as a
destination rather than tacked onto the end of an already long book.
