## Correlation is cheap here

Single-cell data makes correlation almost free. Thousands of genes track with
Alzheimer's pathology, and nearly none of that tells you what would happen if you
actually changed one. The question worth asking is interventional: *if you knocked
this gene down in a microglial cell, what would happen to its disease state?*

**This is not GWAS data.** Readers assume it is, and the assumption changes what
they think the estimand is.

## The estimand

HCGT-PG estimates the causal effect of a gene knockdown on a microglial
disease-state score — and then checks that estimate against CRISPR screens where
the knockdown was really performed. The validation is the point: a causal claim
that cannot be checked against an actual intervention is a correlation with better
vocabulary.

## The data

- **Morabito et al. 2021** — paired snRNA-seq and snATAC-seq multiome.
- **ROSMAP** — cohort-scale expression with neuropathology.
- **Dräger et al. 2022** — CRISPRi/a CROP-seq screens in human microglia, the
  ground truth for the interventional check.
- **Liu et al. 2025** — epigenomic atlas.
