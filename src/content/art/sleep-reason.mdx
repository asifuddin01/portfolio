Plate 43 of _Los Caprichos_, Goya's suite of eighty etchings published in 1799. The artist slumps over his desk while owls and bats rise behind him; the caption is written on the desk itself.

Goya withdrew the series from sale not long after it appeared. The Spanish is _El sueño de la razón produce monstruos_, and _sueño_ carries both senses — sleep and dream — which is why the plate is argued over: reason asleep, or reason dreaming.
