title: "The derivative of a quadratic form"
statement: "∂(xᵀAx)/∂x = (A + Aᵀ)x, which is 2Ax when A is symmetric. ∂(aᵀx)/∂x = a."
shapeCheck: "x is n×1, A is n×n, the form is a scalar, and the gradient is n×1."
workedLine: "A = I, so xᵀAx = ‖x‖² and the gradient is 2x. At x = (1, 2) the gradient is (2, 4)."
status: review

Squared loss, weight decay and the variance of a linear estimator are all quadratic forms, and all differentiate by this line.
