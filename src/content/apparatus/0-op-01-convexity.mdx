statement: "f is convex when f(λx + (1−λ)y) ≤ λf(x) + (1−λ)f(y) for λ ∈ [0, 1]. A twice-differentiable f is convex exactly when its Hessian is positive semi-definite. Every local minimum of a convex function is global."
shapeCheck: "The Hessian of f : ℝⁿ → ℝ is n×n and symmetric."

Almost nothing trained in this corpus is convex. The reason the entry is here is that every intuition about optimisation was formed on convex problems, and it is worth knowing which ones stop applying.
