Alone among the eleven, this one was never interrupted. The Vedic hymns are
recited today; the *Ramayana* and the *Mahabharata* are performed, filmed and
argued over; the Puranas are read as scripture rather than as evidence. Writing
about it in the past tense would be a mistake.

The corpus is enormous and stratified. The Rig Veda's gods are Indra, Agni,
Varuna, Soma — storm, fire, oath and the pressed drink. By the time of the
epics and the Puranas the centre has shifted to Vishnu, Shiva and the Devi,
and the earlier gods are still there but subordinate. The same figure appears
under a dozen names and in a dozen incompatible genealogies, and the tradition
regards this as depth rather than error.

Time is cyclical and vast. Yugas fall into decline and are renewed, kalpas
open and close, and the universe has been made and unmade more times than the
texts trouble to count.
