---
title: Numina
kicker: The divine presences
lede: |-
  Eleven books, read as literature and as evidence. What people said the world was made of before anyone could check, written down — nearly always — long after they had stopped saying it, by someone with reasons of their own.

  I am interested in the seams: where a text contradicts itself and keeps both halves, where a god is plainly two older gods pressed together, where a monk writing in Latin decides what to do with a pagan king. The stories are worth the time on their own. The transmission is worth more.
