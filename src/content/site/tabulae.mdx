---
title: Tabulae
kicker: The plates
lede: |-
  The images interleaved between the chapters. On vellum an engraving is
  printed onto the page rather than pasted on top of it, so the white of the
  original sheet becomes the paper. In the dark it is left as it is — an
  engraving is black lines on white, and inverting one would show you a
  different picture. Each has a note on where it came from.
