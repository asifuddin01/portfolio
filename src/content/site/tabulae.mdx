kicker: The plates
lede: |-
  The images interleaved between the chapters. A visual history of philosophy, from Socrates and Plato to Kant, Nietzsche, Camus, and the thinkers of the modern world. Each tabula explores a philosopher, an idea, a school of thought, or a work of art that shaped the way we understand reason, knowledge, beauty, society, and existence.

  From ancient Athens to the modern world, Tabulae brings philosophy and art together, tracing the thinkers, ideas, and images that have shaped our understanding of what it means to be human.
