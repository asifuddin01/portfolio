title: "ResearchLens — evidence-grounded literature retrieval"
summary: "A research assistant over 101 indexed papers that answers from evidence and refuses when it has none. Structure-preserving PDF parsing — headings by font geometry, two-column reading order recovered from word coverage, de-hyphenation against the document's own vocabulary — then hybrid BM25 and dense retrieval fused by reciprocal rank, and a cross-encoder reranker. Grounding is mechanical rather than requested: retrieval runs first and the model is never called without evidence, citation markers are resolved against the passages actually retrieved, an invented one is deleted before it reaches the reader, and an answer left with nothing supported becomes a refusal. Questions about what is current reach past the corpus to arXiv and PubMed, and those results are marked as abstracts rather than passages, because an abstract supports what a paper claims and not what it measured. Runs locally with no API key; the public demo generates on a free GPU allocation, also without one."
cvSummary: "Evidence-grounded RAG over 101 papers (Python, FastAPI, ONNX, Gradio). Structure-preserving PDF parsing; hybrid BM25 + dense retrieval with RRF fusion and cross-encoder reranking; mechanically enforced grounding — computed citations, fabricated-marker removal, refusal when unsupported; live arXiv and PubMed retrieval for recency; benchmark harness with hand-labelled ground truth. 109 tests, deployed on Hugging Face, runs locally with no API key."
