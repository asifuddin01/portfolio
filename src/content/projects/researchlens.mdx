title: "ResearchLens — evidence-grounded literature retrieval"
summary: "A research assistant over 101 indexed papers that answers from evidence and refuses when it has none. A PDF is not a string: headings are found by font geometry, two-column reading order is recovered from word coverage, and words are de-hyphenated against the document's own vocabulary. Retrieval is hybrid BM25 and dense, fused by reciprocal rank, then reranked by a cross-encoder. Grounding is mechanical rather than requested — retrieval runs first and the model is never called without evidence, citation markers are resolved against the passages actually retrieved, an invented one is deleted before it reaches the reader, and an answer left with nothing supported becomes a refusal. Ask across the whole corpus or confine it to papers you choose; add your own PDFs, which are parsed into an index belonging to your session alone and never merged into the shared one; ask what work exists like a paper you added. Questions about what is current reach past the corpus to arXiv, PubMed and OpenAlex, and those results are marked as abstracts rather than passages, because an abstract supports what a paper claims and not what it measured. Runs locally with no API key at all."
cvSummary: "Evidence-grounded RAG over 101 papers, 9,593 passages (Python, FastAPI, ONNX, Gradio). Structure-preserving PDF parsing; hybrid BM25 + dense retrieval with RRF fusion and cross-encoder reranking; mechanically enforced grounding — computed citations, fabricated-marker removal, refusal when unsupported. Per-session document upload isolated from the shared index; subset-scoped retrieval; related-work discovery across arXiv, PubMed and OpenAlex; benchmark harness with hand-labelled ground truth. 162 tests, deployed on Hugging Face, runs locally with no API key."
