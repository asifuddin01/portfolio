## The aim

Predict when food items, such as fruit, vegetables and packaged goods, will expire, to cut food waste. The project's full title is *Leveraging CNN and Random Forest for Accurate Food Expiry Prediction*.

## How it works

- **Images.** A convolutional neural network analyses photographs for freshness.
- **Conditions.** Random Forest and multi-layer perceptron models process environmental metadata: temperature, humidity and storage duration.

## Data

Built on the Kaggle dataset *Fruits Fresh and Rotten for Classification*, alongside self-generated features such as days since purchase, to improve generalisation.
