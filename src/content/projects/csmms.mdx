## What it does

The FastAPI backend for a campus student management system and marketplace.

- **Marketplace items.** Create, read, update and delete, with keyword, category, price-range and sort filtering, and pagination.
- **Images.** Uploaded to S3 with Pillow compression and delivered through CloudFront.
- **Services.** Priority levels and impact metrics computed live: weighted student ratings, students served and completion rate.

## Built with

FastAPI, SQLAlchemy models, JWT authentication and Alembic migrations, with images on AWS S3 and CloudFront.
