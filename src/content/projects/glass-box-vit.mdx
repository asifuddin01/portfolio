title: "Glass-Box ViT — a Vision Transformer rebuilt from scratch and verified layer by layer against a pretrained ViT-Base"
summary: "Vision Transformers are usually used through a library, so their insides stay a black box. This one is rebuilt from basic PyTorch layers — patch projection, learned positions, a CLS token, packed QKV with a hand-written softmax(QKᵀ/√d)V, and pre-norm blocks — and then shown to be the same function as a production pretrained model, not merely to agree on its top-1 class. A strict mapping loads all 152 tensors, 86,567,656 parameters, of the pinned timm checkpoint vit_base_patch16_224.augreg_in21k_ft_in1k, with its revision and SHA-256 verified, and refuses any missing key or shape mismatch. Compared stage by stage, it matches the pretrained model exactly at all 100 intermediate stages (max difference 0.0), on an Apple M1 CPU and on a Colab Tesla T4. At 224 px and batch 1, inference takes 81.0 ms on the CPU and 15.0 ms on the T4, against 14.6 ms for timm's fused attention. The same model then measures which design choices matter, in CIFAR-10 ablations over three paired seeds that share identical splits, with each protocol frozen and committed before its run. Patch size matters most: 71.6%, 63.4% and 55.9% validation accuracy for patches of 4, 8 and 16 at width 96, and 75.3%, 68.7% and 59.3% at width 192. Learned position embeddings beat none in every seed, 63.4% against 56.0%; mean-patch pooling beats CLS pooling in every seed, 65.2% against 63.4%; and 4, 8, 12 or 16 heads at fixed width make no clear difference, 63.8, 63.4, 63.0 and 62.9%. These are validation accuracies of small models trained from scratch for 20 epochs, and the test split was never used. A RetinaMNIST transfer study, a research and education exercise rather than a clinical tool, scores test QWK 0.750 with a linear probe, 0.775 ± 0.038 with partial fine-tuning and 0.773 ± 0.038 with full fine-tuning: neither fine-tuning method beat the probe reliably. Its models were selected on validation data only, with test metrics recomputed from saved predictions and bootstrap 95% intervals. Every trained checkpoint was reloaded and shown to reproduce its saved validation predictions, and two GPU-only bugs, a device mismatch in that reload check and a CPU data-loading bottleneck, were found and fixed before the GPU numbers were trusted. Last-layer attention maps are shown as diagnostics, not explanations: CLS attention concentrates on a few background patches, a known ViT artifact. A clean clone installs and passes all 34 tests."
