title: "Winnow — a free, self-hostable platform for systematic reviews"
summary: "The tools a research group needs to run a systematic review are commercial and closed, and unpublished review data has to be uploaded to them. Winnow is the whole pipeline instead, free and open source, on a server the lab owns: twenty search exports in at once (RIS, PubMed MEDLINE and XML, BibTeX, EndNote XML, Zotero RDF, CSV), automatic deduplication with the uncertain pairs shown side by side for a person, then keyboard-first blind or open screening by two reviewers with conflict resolution, full texts uploaded or fetched from Unpaywall and PubMed Central and virus-scanned before anyone opens them, versioned extraction forms with dual extraction and a consensus step, risk of bias in RoB 2, ROBINS-I, Newcastle–Ottawa or QUADAS-2 with traffic-light plots, and out the other end the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram, Cohen's and Fleiss' kappa, a methods paragraph to paste, and a backup that restores anywhere. A relevance model retrains from each reviewer's own decisions, puts the likely includes first and estimates when stopping is defensible; the AI suggestions are optional, because a review that cannot say why a record was excluded is not a review. Security is server-side or it does not count: every query is checked against review membership and a non-member gets a 404, PostgreSQL row-level security sits behind that as a second line, and blind mode is enforced in the database rather than hidden in the interface — alongside Argon2id, two-factor sign-in, account lockout, Google and ORCID sign-in, an append-only audit log, and an OWASP ZAP baseline scan in CI with no high-risk findings. Every performance number was written as a budget first and then measured on 100,000-record reviews: screening 32–69 ms at the 95th percentile against 80, the record list 12–64 ms across fifteen filters, sorts and searches against 150, an import of 100,000 records in 49 s against 60, deduplication of 50,000 in 13 s against 30, and a 0.7 s LCP on 4G against 1.5 — with 50 reviewers deciding every three seconds for ten minutes giving a 37 ms 95th percentile and 0 errors in 19,920 requests. WCAG 2.2 AA with zero serious or critical axe findings across 43 pages, four screen sizes and both themes; 900 backend tests at 95% coverage, Playwright end to end, mypy --strict and TypeScript strict, CI on every push; one command brings up the production stack with automatic HTTPS, encrypted nightly backups and a restore test CI runs monthly. Not live yet, and for an honest reason: it runs its own database, background worker and virus scanner, so it needs a paid server rather than free static hosting. It goes up at winnow.sandhiresearch.org for Sandhi Research Lab once that server is in place."
