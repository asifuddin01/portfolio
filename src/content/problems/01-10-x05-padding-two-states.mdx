An LSTM coordinate has $c=1$ before a padded step. During padding the input is
zero and the gates are $f=0.5$, $i=0.5$, $o=0.75$ with candidate $0.6$. The
next real step has $f=0.5$, $i=0.5$, candidate $0$ and $o=0.75$. Compare the
next real hidden state when the padded step updates $c$ but freezes $h$, and
when it freezes both.

<Hint>
Freezing $h$ alone still runs the cell update.
</Hint>

<SolutionToggle>

Freezing only $h$: the padded step computes $c=0.5\cdot1+0.5\cdot0.6=0.8$.
The next real step gives $c=0.5\cdot0.8=0.4$ and
$h=0.75\tanh(0.4)=0.284962$.

Freezing both: the cell stays at $1$, the next step gives $c=0.5$ and
$h=0.75\tanh(0.5)=0.346588$.

The masked update of I.9 applied to $\vec h$ alone is not enough for an LSTM.
Mask both states: if $\vec c_t^{\,\text{new}}$ is the cell update the step
computed, keep $m_t\vec c_t^{\,\text{new}}+(1-m_t)\vec c_{t-1}$, and apply the
same rule to $\vec h_t$. Or pack the sequence so padded steps never run.

</SolutionToggle>
