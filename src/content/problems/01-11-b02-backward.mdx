<Statement>
Derive and check the kernel, input and bias gradients of I.11.B01.
</Statement>
<Given>
Use I.11.B01's $X$ and $K$. The upstream derivative is

$$
G=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\-1&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Define the scalar objective $F=\sum_{i,j}G_{i,j}Y_{i,j}$.
Treat $G$ as fixed during differentiation.
</Given>
<Find>
The gradient formulas and their complete numerical arrays.
</Find>
<Strategy>
Differentiate one forward product, then sum every occurrence of each shared
weight or overlapping input. Confirm the result using central differences.
</Strategy>
<Solution>

The differential of one output is

$$
\begin{aligned}
 &dY_{i,j}=db\\
 &\quad+\sum_{u,v}X_{i+u,j+v}\,dK_{u,v}\\
 &\quad+\sum_{u,v}K_{u,v}\,dX_{i+u,j+v}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Multiplying by $G_{i,j}$ and summing gives (I.11.3).
For the kernel entries, the sums are

$$
\begin{aligned}
dK_{0,0}&=1(1)+2(2)\\
 &\quad-1(0)+1(1)=6,\\
dK_{0,1}&=1(2)+2(0)\\
 &\quad-1(1)+1(3)=4,\\
dK_{1,0}&=1(0)+2(1)\\
 &\quad-1(2)+1(1)=1,\\
dK_{1,1}&=1(1)+2(3)\\
 &\quad-1(1)+1(0)=6.
\end{aligned}
$$

The bias derivative is $1+2-1+1=3$.
For the input, scatter each $G_{i,j}K$ into its corresponding window
and add overlaps:

$$
dX=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\-2&1&4\\1&-3&2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

The centre illustrates why assignment is wrong.
Its four contributions are $2$, $-2$, $0$ and $1$, whose sum is one.

The forward objective is $F=1(3)+2(7)-1(0)+1(0)=17$.
With zero bias, the map is linear in $X$ while $K$ is fixed,
and linear in $K$ while $X$ is fixed. Consequently,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle dX,X\rangle&=17,\\
\langle dK,K\rangle&=17.
\end{aligned}
$$

These dot products are useful independent checks.
The script also perturbs every input, weight and bias by $10^{-6}$ and
compares central differences to the analytic result within $10^{-7}$.
Additional tests use padding, stride and dilation, where the same
scatter-add rule must follow a different index map.

</Solution>
<Answer>
$dK=[[6,4],[1,6]]$, $db=3$ and
$dX=[[1,2,0],[-2,1,4],[1,-3,2]]$.
</Answer>
<CheckSanity>
The input-gradient shape matches $X$ and the weight-gradient shape matches
$K$. All three occurrences of a nonzero centre contribution are retained.
</CheckSanity>
<Breaks>
This is the derivative of the stated cross-correlation. Introducing another
kernel flip while scattering would compute a different derivative.
A nonlinear activation after the convolution must be differentiated first.
</Breaks>
<Variation>
If the loss is averaged over the four outputs rather than summed, divide
every upstream entry by four. All three gradient arrays scale accordingly;
do not divide the already-scaled gradients again.
</Variation>
