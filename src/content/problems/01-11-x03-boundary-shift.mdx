Cross-correlate $(1,2,3)$ with $(1,1,1)$ using zero padding one on both
sides and stride one. Compare correlating its cropped right shift $(0,1,2)$
with taking the cropped right shift of its original output.

<Hint>
A cropped right shift discards the last entry and inserts zero on the left.
</Hint>
<SolutionToggle>

The original output is $(3,6,5)$.
Correlating the shifted input gives $(1,3,3)$.
Shifting the original output gives $(0,3,6)$.
The two disagree at the boundary positions.

This does not contradict I.11.T1: the finite crop removes values that an
unrestricted-grid translation would retain. A boundary convention is part of
the operator, not an implementation detail outside its mathematics.
Circular shifts with compatible circular padding give a different case.

</SolutionToggle>
