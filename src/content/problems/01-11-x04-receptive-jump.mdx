Track receptive-field span and sampling jump through three width-three
layers with strides $(1,2,1)$ and dilation one. All use symmetric padding one.
What are the first-output centre and final jump if original pixel centres
start at $1/2$?

<Hint>
Use the preceding layer's jump when growing the span.
</Hint>
<SolutionToggle>

Starting from $(R_0,J_0)=(1,1)$, (I.11.6) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
(R_1,J_1)&=(3,1),\\
(R_2,J_2)&=(5,2),\\
(R_3,J_3)&=(9,2).
\end{aligned}
$$

Every layer's centre increment is
$((3-1)/2-1)J_{\mathrm{previous}}=0$.
The first centre remains $1/2$; neighbouring final outputs are two original
pixels apart. At the boundary, part of the nominal nine-position span is padding.

Updating the second jump before computing its span would give seven
instead of five at layer two. The stride changes spacing between outputs,
not the taps inside that layer's first window.

</SolutionToggle>
