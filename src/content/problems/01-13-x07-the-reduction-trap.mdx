A VAE for $28\times28$ images in $[0,1]$ has a batch of $B$ inputs of
shape $(B,1,28,28)$, a code of width $k=32$, and a Gaussian decoder with
$\sigma^2=\tfrac12$, so that $-\log p(\vec x\mid\vec z)$ is the summed
squared error plus a constant. Give the shape of every tensor from the
encoder's output to the loss. Then compare the correct loss with the one
written as the mean squared error over all elements plus the KL summed
over the code and averaged over the batch. What $\beta$ does the second
one train with, and by I.13.T3 what happens to the code?

<Hint>
A mean over all elements divides by $B\cdot784$. The KL was divided only
by $B$.
</Hint>

<SolutionToggle>

**Shapes.** The encoder's last layer returns $(B,64)$, split into
$\vec\mu$ and $\log\vec\sigma^2$, each $(B,32)$. The noise
$\vec\varepsilon$ and the code $\vec z$ are $(B,32)$. The decoder returns
$(B,784)$, compared with the input flattened to $(B,784)$. The KL by
(I.13.5) is summed over the $32$ coordinates to give $(B,)$, as is the
squared error over the $784$ pixels, and the loss averages both over the
batch.

**The two losses.** The correct negative ELBO, up to a constant, is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac1B\sum_b\Big(&\sum_{i=1}^{784}(x_{bi}-\hat x_{bi})^2\\
&+\sum_{j=1}^{32}\KL_{bj}\Big).
\end{aligned}
$$

The second divides the squared error by $B\cdot784$ and the KL by $B$.
Multiplying it by $784$ gives the correct reconstruction term plus $784$
times the KL. The two have the same minimisers only if $\beta=784$.

**What happens.** In the linear model of I.13.T3 a direction survives only
if its variance exceeds $\beta\sigma^2=392$. A pixel in $[0,1]$ has
variance at most $\tfrac14$, so the total variance of all $784$ pixels is
at most $196$, and no direction exceeds that. Every direction collapses:
the encoder returns the prior and the decoder learns the mean image.

A nonlinear VAE is not bound by this exactly, but the pressure is the
same, and blurred reconstructions with a KL near zero are the usual
symptom. The shapes were all correct. Only the reductions disagreed.

</SolutionToggle>
