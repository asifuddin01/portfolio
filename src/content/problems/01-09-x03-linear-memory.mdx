For $h_t=wh_{t-1}+ux_t$ with fixed $h_0$, derive a closed form for $h_T$
and for $\partial h_T/\partial w$. Verify the derivative at
$T=3$, $h_0=0$, $u=1$, $w=1/2$, and $(x_1,x_2,x_3)=(1,2,0)$.

<Hint>
Expand two steps, identify the coefficient of each input, and differentiate
the resulting polynomial.
</Hint>

<SolutionToggle>

Each earlier input is multiplied by $w$ once for every remaining step:

$$
h_T=w^Th_0+u\sum_{s=1}^T w^{T-s}x_s.
$$

The formula follows by induction: multiplying the expression for $T-1$
by $w$ raises each exponent, and adding $ux_T$ supplies the final term.

For integer $T\geq1$,

$$
\frac{\partial h_T}{\partial w}
=Tw^{T-1}h_0+
u\sum_{s=1}^{T-1}(T-s)w^{T-s-1}x_s.
$$

The last input's derivative is omitted rather than written as zero times
a negative power. This polynomial formula is valid at $w=0$ too, using
the ordinary constant monomial $w^0=1$.

For the specified values, $h_3=w^2+2w=1.25$ and its derivative is
$2w+2=3$. With readout $z=2h_3$ and target one, multiplying by
$(z-1)2=3$ gives the loss derivative nine from I.9.B02.

The closed form relies on a linear transition with constant parameters.
Tanh prevents this distributive expansion.

</SolutionToggle>
