Compute the output length for input seven, kernel three, dilation one,
padding one on each side and stride two. List padded-coordinate origins.
Then choose padding for a width-four, stride-one kernel to retain length seven.

<Hint>
Use the effective span, and require the last tap to stay inside the padded input.
</Hint>
<SolutionToggle>

Equation (I.11.4) gives
$\lfloor(7+2-3)/2\rfloor+1=4$.
In a padded input of length nine, origins $0,2,4,6$ end at $2,4,6,8$.
Origin eight would end at ten and does not fit.

For width four at stride one, total padding must be three.
Splits $(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$ both preserve length but shift alignment
differently. Equal integer padding cannot sum to three.
The phrase 'same size' does not determine which side gets the extra value.

</SolutionToggle>
