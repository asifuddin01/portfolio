A scalar tanh cell has current output $h_t=0.6$, recurrent weight $w=0.5$,
and total upstream state derivative $q_t=5$. Compute $\delta_t$ and the
backward contribution to $h_{t-1}$. Repeat for $h_t=0.99$.

<Hint>
Use $1-h_t^2$, then multiply by $w$ to cross the recurrent edge.
</Hint>

<SolutionToggle>

For $h_t=0.6$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta_t&=5(1-0.6^2)=3.2,\\
\partial_{h_{t-1}}\mathcal L\big|_{\text{via }t}
 &=0.5(3.2)=1.6.
\end{aligned}
$$

For $h_t=0.99$, the activation factor is $0.0199$.
Thus $\delta_t=0.0995$ and the returned contribution is $0.04975$.
The weight and upstream signal are unchanged; saturation alone
greatly reduces what travels back.

If the earlier state has its own local loss, that separate derivative
must also be added. These numbers describe the path through step $t$,
not every possible contribution to the earlier state.

</SolutionToggle>
