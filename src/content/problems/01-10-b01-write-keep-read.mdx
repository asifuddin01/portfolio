<Statement>
Run a scalar LSTM through three inputs and separate what the input gate writes
from what the forget gate keeps.
</Statement>

<Given>
A scalar cell follows (I.10.1) and (I.10.2) with every recurrent weight zero.
The gates read only their biases: $b_i=0$, $b_f=\ln3$, $b_o=\ln3$, and
$u_i=u_f=u_o=0$. The candidate is $\tilde c_t=\tanh(x_t\ln3)$, so
$u_c=\ln3$ and $b_c=0$. Start from $c_0=h_0=0$ and feed $(1,0,0)$.
</Given>

<Find>
The three gate values, the candidates, all three cell and hidden states, the
third cell state when $b_f=\ln99$ instead, and the number of trainable scalars.
</Find>

<Strategy>
Evaluate the gates first; they are constants here. Then apply the cell update
one step at a time and read each hidden state through the output gate.
</Strategy>

<Solution>

Since $\sigma(\ln a)=a/(1+a)$, the gates are $i=\sigma(0)=1/2$ and
$f=o=\sigma(\ln3)=3/4$. The first candidate is

$$
\tanh(\ln3)=\frac{3-1/3}{3+1/3}=\frac45,
$$

and the later candidates are $\tanh(0)=0$.

The cell update writes once and then only keeps:

$$
\begin{aligned}
c_1&=\tfrac34\cdot0+\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac45=0.4,\\
c_2&=\tfrac34\cdot0.4+\tfrac12\cdot0=0.3,\\
c_3&=\tfrac34\cdot0.3=0.225.
\end{aligned}
$$

The hidden states are $h_t=\tfrac34\tanh(c_t)$:
$0.284962$, $0.218484$ and $0.165959$.

With $b_f=\ln99$ the forget gate is $0.99$, and
$c_3=0.4\cdot0.99^2=0.39204$. The input gate decided how much was written;
the forget gate alone decided how much of it survived two silent steps.

Each of the four blocks has one input weight, one recurrent weight and one
bias, so the scalar cell has $12$ trainable scalars. The plain scalar cell of
[I.9.B01](/elementa/neural-networks/recurrence/problems/b01) had three before
its readout.

</Solution>

<Answer>
Gates $(i,f,o)=(1/2,3/4,3/4)$; candidates $(0.8,0,0)$; cell states
$(0.4,0.3,0.225)$; hidden states $(0.284962,0.218484,0.165959)$; with
$f=0.99$, $c_3=0.39204$; twelve trainable scalars.
</Answer>

<CheckSanity>
Each hidden state is smaller than its cell state. For $c>0$,
$\tanh c<c$, and the output gate multiplies by a further three quarters.
The hidden state is a view of the cell, not a copy of it.
</CheckSanity>

<Breaks>
Nonzero recurrent weights make the gates depend on $h_{t-1}$, so they change
from step to step and the cell states are no longer a geometric sequence.
The write-then-keep reading survives; the clean numbers do not.
</Breaks>

<Variation>
Feed $(1,1,0)$ instead. The second step keeps $0.3$ and writes another $0.4$,
so $c_2=0.7$ and $c_3=0.525$. The cell accumulates; it does not overwrite
unless the forget gate is small.
</Variation>
