For $y=x+F(x)$ take $F(x)=-x$. Compute the output and Jacobian.
Now consider a residual branch changing shape from $(N,16,32,32)$ to
$(N,32,16,16)$. Can it be added directly to the input?

<Hint>
Differentiate the sum, and check every axis before adding tensors.
</Hint>
<SolutionToggle>

The output is zero and the Jacobian is $I-I=0$.
Thus the identity term in (I.11.8) does not give a positive lower bound
on the total gradient. It creates a route that can help training,
but another route can cancel it.

The second addition is invalid: channel and spatial dimensions differ.
One possible shortcut is a $1$ by $1$ projection from 16 to 32 channels
with stride two and no padding. It produces $(N,32,16,16)$.
Its derivative is the projection's Jacobian rather than an identity.
The alignment of the two branches still needs checking.
