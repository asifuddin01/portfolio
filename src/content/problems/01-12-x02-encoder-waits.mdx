For the four points of
[I.12.B01](/elementa/neural-networks/autoencoders/problems/b01),
$\mat S=\begin{bmatrix}2.92&1.44\\1.44&2.08\end{bmatrix}$. At
$\mat E=(1,\,0)$ and $\mat D=(1,\,0)^{\mathsf T}$, evaluate both gradients
of (I.12.2). Take one step of gradient descent with step size $0.1$ and
find the new loss. Then evaluate both gradients at $\mat E=\mat 0$,
$\mat D=\mat 0$.

<Hint>
Form $(\mat I-\mat D\mat E)\mat S$ once and use it in both gradients.
</Hint>

<SolutionToggle>

Here $\mat I-\mat D\mat E=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$, so

$$
(\mat I-\mat D\mat E)\mat S=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\1.44&2.08\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Multiplying on the right by $\mat E^{\mathsf T}=(1,0)^{\mathsf T}$ and on
the left by $\mat D^{\mathsf T}=(1,\,0)$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_{\mat D}\mathcal L&=-2\begin{bmatrix}0\\1.44\end{bmatrix}
 =\begin{bmatrix}0\\-2.88\end{bmatrix},\\
\nabla_{\mat E}\mathcal L&=-2\,(0,\;0)=(0,\;0).
\end{aligned}
$$

The decoder is told to raise its second weight: the residual, which lives
in the second coordinate, is correlated with the code. The encoder is told
nothing. Its gradient passes through $\mat D^{\mathsf T}$, and the decoder
does not yet write to the coordinate where the error is.

After the step, $\mat D=(1,\,0.288)^{\mathsf T}$ and $\mat E$ is unchanged.
The residual map is $\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\-0.288&1\end{bmatrix}$ and the loss
is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&0.288^2\cdot2.92-2\cdot0.288\cdot1.44+2.08\\
&\;\;=1.492756,
\end{aligned}
$$

down from $2.08$. On the next step the encoder's gradient is no longer
zero.

At $\mat E=\mat 0$ and $\mat D=\mat 0$, each gradient has a zero matrix as
a factor, so both vanish. The loss is $\operatorname{tr}\mat S=5$ and
gradient descent never leaves. An autoencoder needs a nonzero
initialisation in both halves.

</SolutionToggle>
