<Statement>
Three tokens attend to one another under scaled dot-product attention. Compute
the whole forward pass by hand and confirm that every attention row is a
probability distribution.
</Statement>

<Given>
$$
\mat{Q} = \mat{K} = \mat{V} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
\in \R^{3 \times 2}, \qquad \dk = 2 .
$$

Row $i$ of each matrix belongs to token $i$. Row-major throughout, as fixed in
the Apparatus.
</Given>

<Find>
The score matrix $\mat{S}$, the scaled scores $\mat{Z}$, the attention weights
$\mat{A}$, and the output $\mat{O}$ — each a $3 \times 3$ matrix except
$\mat{O}$, which is $3 \times 2$.
</Find>

<Strategy>
Name each intermediate rather than nesting them, so a wrong digit can be traced
to the step that produced it.
</Strategy>

<Solution>

**Step 1 — the scores.** $\mat{S} = \mat{Q}\mat{K}^{\mathsf T}$, so $S_{ij}$ is
the inner product of query row $i$ with key row $j$.

$$
\mat{S} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}
$$

Token 3's query is $(1,1)$, which agrees with both basis directions, so its row
is the largest. Nothing about position enters: $S_{ij}$ is a function of content
alone.

**Step 2 — the scaling.** Divide by $\sqrt{\dk} = \sqrt 2 \approx 1.4142$.

$$
\mat{Z} = \begin{bmatrix}
0.7071 & 0 & 0.7071 \\
0 & 0.7071 & 0.7071 \\
0.7071 & 0.7071 & 1.4142
\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Step 3 — the softmax, row by row.** Take row 1, $\vec{z} = (0.7071, 0, 0.7071)$.
Subtract the row maximum first, which changes nothing and removes the overflow
(<ApparatusRef ref="0.NU.02" />):

$$
\vec{z} - \max = (0,\; -0.7071,\; 0), \qquad
e^{0} = 1, \quad e^{-0.7071} = 0.4931 .
$$

The denominator is $1 + 0.4931 + 1 = 2.4931$, so

$$
\vec{a}_1 = \left(\tfrac{1}{2.4931},\; \tfrac{0.4931}{2.4931},\; \tfrac{1}{2.4931}\right)
= (0.4011,\; 0.1978,\; 0.4011) .
$$

Row 2 is row 1 with the first two entries exchanged, by the symmetry of the
inputs. Row 3 has $\vec{z} = (0.7071, 0.7071, 1.4142)$; subtracting the maximum
gives $(-0.7071, -0.7071, 0)$, exponentials $0.4931, 0.4931, 1$, denominator
$1.9862$, and

$$
\vec{a}_3 = (0.2483,\; 0.2483,\; 0.5035) .
$$

**Step 4 — the aggregation.** $\mat{O} = \mat{A}\mat{V}$. For row 1, column 1:

$$
O_{11} = 0.4011(1) + 0.1978(0) + 0.4011(1) = 0.8022 ,
$$

and column 2 is $0.4011(0) + 0.1978(1) + 0.4011(1) = 0.5989$.

</Solution>

<Answer>
$$
\mat{A} = \begin{bmatrix}
0.4011 & 0.1978 & 0.4011 \\
0.1978 & 0.4011 & 0.4011 \\
0.2483 & 0.2483 & 0.5035
\end{bmatrix}, \qquad
\mat{O} = \begin{bmatrix}
0.8022 & 0.5989 \\
0.5989 & 0.8022 \\
0.7517 & 0.7517
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$\mat{A}$ is $3 \times 3$ and dimensionless; $\mat{O}$ is $3 \times 2$ and
carries the units of $\mat{V}$.
</Answer>

<CheckNumeric snippet="i-5-b01-attention-by-hand.py" verified>
```python
S = [[sum(q[i] * k[i] for i in range(d_k)) for k in K] for q in Q]
Z = [[s / sqrt(d_k) for s in row] for row in S]
A = [softmax(row) for row in Z]
O = [[sum(a[j] * V[j][c] for j in range(3)) for c in range(2)] for a in A]
```

The full twenty-line script is `scripts/snippets/i-5-b01-attention-by-hand.py`,
and its output is diffed against the digits above on every build.
</CheckNumeric>

<CheckSanity>
Three independent reasons the answer is right.

**Rows sum to one.** $0.4011 + 0.1978 + 0.4011 = 1.0000$, and likewise for the
other two. A softmax row that does not sum to one is an arithmetic slip, not a
modelling choice.

**Every output lies inside the convex hull of $\mat{V}$.** The value rows are
$(1,0)$, $(0,1)$ and $(1,1)$; every entry of $\mat{O}$ lies in $[0,1]$, as a
convex combination must. This is the whole content of Proposition 1: attention
mixes, and cannot leave the hull.

**The symmetry survives.** $\mat{Q} = \mat{K}$ here, so $\mat{S}$ is symmetric,
and rows 1 and 2 of the answer are exchanged copies of one another. They are.
</CheckSanity>

<Breaks>
The convex-hull argument holds only because the weights are non-negative and
sum to one. Remove the softmax — score-weighted sums, or normalisation by
anything other than the total — and the output can leave the hull entirely.
That is why the feed-forward block after attention is not decoration.
</Breaks>
