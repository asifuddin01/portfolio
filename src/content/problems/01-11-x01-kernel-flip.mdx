For input $(1,2,3)$ and kernel $(1,2)$, compute valid stride-one
cross-correlation, then repeat after reversing the kernel.

<Hint>
The windows are $(1,2)$ and $(2,3)$.
</Hint>
<SolutionToggle>

Cross-correlation gives $(1+4,2+6)=(5,8)$.
Reversing the kernel to $(2,1)$ gives $(2+2,4+3)=(4,7)$.
The second corresponds to the flipped-kernel convention on the same valid
windows. Neither answer is a numerical approximation to the other.

A learned filter can be reversed through its learned parameters, so this
does not restrict the family of learnable linear maps. But fixed filters,
checkpoints and hand calculations must agree on orientation.

</SolutionToggle>
