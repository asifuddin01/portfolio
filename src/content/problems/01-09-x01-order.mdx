For $h_t=x_t+wh_{t-1}$ with $h_0=0$ and $w=1/2$, compare the final
states for inputs $(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$. Derive when two inputs $(a,b)$
can be swapped without changing the final state.

<Hint>
Write the two final states in terms of $a$, $b$ and $w$ before substituting.
</Hint>

<SolutionToggle>

The forward order gives $h_2=2+(1/2)1=2.5$.
The reverse gives $h_2=1+(1/2)2=2$.

Generally the difference is

$$
(b+wa)-(a+wb)=(1-w)(b-a).
$$

It is zero when $a=b$ or $w=1$. With $w=1$, this particular linear
cell sums its inputs, so order is lost. For other $w$, sharing the
transition does not make the representation permutation-invariant.
Even $w=0$ retains the final input and therefore distinguishes these
two orders.

A nonlinear recurrent cell has a different equality condition; do not
transfer the special sum-cell result to every RNN.

</SolutionToggle>
