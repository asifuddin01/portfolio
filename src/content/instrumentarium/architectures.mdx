Heads and fusion: transformer decoders with learned label queries, gated cross-attention between masks and images, frozen image-text encoders with a trained fusion module. Language and vision-language models: Qwen3-VL-2B fine-tuned with QLoRA, Qwen2.5-7B with QLoRA and Qwen2.5-3B with LoRA; gpt-oss-120b, Qwen2.5 Coder 32B and Qwen2.5 3B served in deployed systems. Retrieval and speech: BGE embeddings, MiniLM cross-encoder reranking, multilingual MiniLM embeddings, Whisper large-v3, Kokoro-82M.'
