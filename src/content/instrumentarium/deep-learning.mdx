Inference: test-time augmentation with re-tuned thresholds, probability ensembling with weights chosen on validation. Building blocks: ordinal regression (CORN), mixture-of-experts, cross-attention fusion, attention gates, ASPP, GeM pooling, deep supervision, Grad-CAM. Numerical checks: layer-by-layer equivalence against reference weights, and finding a bf16 softmax that collapsed scores."
