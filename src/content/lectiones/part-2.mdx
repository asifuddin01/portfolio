Parts VII through XII are all modifications of the architecture in the first paper here. It is worth more of your time than anything else on this list.

Read BERT and GPT-1 as a pair. They were published four months apart, they use the same building block, and they differ in one decision: whether a token may see the tokens after it. Everything that follows — why one model fills in blanks and the other continues text, why one became an encoder and the other became the industry — comes out of that single choice about the mask.

Then GPT-3, which is seventy-five pages long and whose finding is in the first ten.
