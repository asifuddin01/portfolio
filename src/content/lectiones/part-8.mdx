A short part, because the story is short and unusually clean.

The first paper proposes training a small number of inserted parameters instead of all of them, two years before anyone urgently needed it. The second finds a better shape for those parameters, one that can be folded back into the weights so inference costs nothing extra. The third quantises everything around it so the whole procedure fits on hardware you can rent by the hour.

Read them in order. LoRA reads as an invention on its own and as a refinement next to adapters, and the second reading is the accurate one.
