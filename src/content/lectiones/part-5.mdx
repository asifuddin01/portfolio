The first paper here makes a claim with a condition attached, and the condition is the interesting part: a plain Transformer beats convolution on images *given enough pre-training data*, and below roughly a hundred million images it loses.

The next four papers are all, in one way or another, attacks on that condition. Swin puts a useful inductive bias back. DeiT shows the data requirement was an artefact of the training recipe. MAE and DINOv2 remove the labels instead of the data.

Read Part I again if the convolution baselines are hazy. The comparison is the point.
