# Gilgamesh chapter images

Prepared 2026-10-04. All three files are photographs of ancient objects,
not generated illustrations. Originals were proportionally resized to a
maximum dimension of 1280 pixels and encoded as WebP at quality 86.
No cropping, compositing, or retouching was applied.

## combat-seal.webp

- Object: cylinder seal and modern impression, heroes and animals in combat,
  with a head identified as probably Humbaba. Old Babylonian, ca. 2000 to
  1600 BCE. The Met, 1999.325.24.
- Record: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/327620
- Source: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/an/original/DP-36510-001.jpg
- Credit: Gift of Nanette B. Kelekian, in memory of Charles Dikran and
  Beatrice Kelekian, 1999.
- Image: The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Public domain, confirmed by the
  museum's collection API `isPublicDomain` field.
- Interpretive limit: the figures are not securely identified as Gilgamesh
  and Enkidu; the impression alongside the ancient seal is modern.

## humbaba-head.webp

- Photograph: Rama, Wikimedia Commons.
- File page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Head_of_Humbaba-AO_6778-IMG_0661-b-white.jpg
- Source: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ab/Head_of_Humbaba-AO_6778-IMG_0661-b-white.jpg
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 France.
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/fr/deed.en
- Changes: proportional resize and WebP conversion only. This image
  version is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 France licence.
- Interpretive limit: an image of the guardian's face, not a depiction
  of his death and not a portrait of Gilgamesh.

## flood-tablet.webp

- Object: Flood Tablet, Tablet XI of the Gilgamesh epic, seventh century
  BCE, Nineveh, library of Ashurbanipal. British Museum, K.3375.
- Record: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_K-3375
- Required photo credit: Photograph by Mike Peel (www.mikepeel.net).
- File page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:British_Museum_Flood_Tablet_1.jpg
- Source: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5b/British_Museum_Flood_Tablet_1.jpg
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Changes: proportional resize and WebP conversion only. This image
  version is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
- Interpretive limit: one fragmentary witness to the flood account,
  not the whole epic.
