The Heh amulet shows the god whose name is the word for unending time; it is not a figure of the Eight, who share that word in one of their names. The pyramidion's sun on a scarab is one face of four; the museum describes the others. Neither object carries a creation text, and the chapter's captions say so. The Ptah figure is dated by its style, not by an inscription. The Amun statuette's Karnak findspot is reported, not excavated.
