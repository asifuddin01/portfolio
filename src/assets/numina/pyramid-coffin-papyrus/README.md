The coffin view shows exterior decoration, not an edition of the Coffin Texts. Nauny's image shows a detail, not the complete roll. Sobekmose's image shows a museum installation of parts of a New Kingdom official's chamber, not an Old Kingdom royal pyramid; other walls are in Boston. The chapter captions preserve these distinctions.
