# Ma'at: image credits

All four images are public-domain Open Access photographs from The Metropolitan Museum of Art. The official Collection API returned `isPublicDomain: true` for each object on 2026-10-12. Local WebP copies are resized so the longest side is at most 1400px, without cropping or retouching. Astro generates responsive sizes. No reconstructed or generated imagery is presented as an ancient object.

The ostracon is drawn on both sides; only the side with the offering of Maat is shown, and its attribution to the draftsman Amenhotep is the museum's. The Maat figure has lost its feather, which the museum infers from traces on the head, and has no recorded findspot. The Hatshepsut statue was reassembled from fragments and its gaps are filled with tinted modern plaster; it is the inscription, not the jars she holds, that names the offering as Maat. The stela of Mentuwoser is his own account of himself, and its Abydos origin is the museum's "probably".
